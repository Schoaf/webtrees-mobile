# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-09-24**

This app ("Stammbaum App" / webtrees-mobile) is a companion client for a self-hosted [webtrees](https://webtrees.net) genealogy website. It does not operate its own backend, does not run analytics or advertising, and does not collect, store, or transmit any data to the app developer or any third party.

## How the app handles data

The app only exchanges data directly with the webtrees server you (or whoever set up the app) configured it to connect to. All genealogical data — names, dates, places, photos, notes, and your account (username, password, session) — is stored and processed exclusively on that server, under that server's own privacy policy and the control of its administrator.

**The developer of this app has no access to that server or its data.** Because every installation can point at a different, independently operated server, this document cannot describe that server's specific data practices — ask your tree's administrator for their privacy policy, or check the server's own webtrees installation for one.

## What stays on your device

- **Login session**: after signing in, a session token is stored in the operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) so you don't have to log in every time. It never leaves your device except to authenticate with the server you configured.
- **Optional biometric app lock**: if you enable Face ID / fingerprint unlock, that preference is stored locally. Biometric data itself is handled entirely by the operating system and never reaches this app or any server.
- **Camera / photo library access**: only used, with your explicit permission at the time, to attach or update a photo for a person in your tree. Photos you choose are uploaded only to the server you're connected to.
- No data described above is shared with the developer, with each other's installations, or with any third party.

## Third-party services

The app does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

## Children's privacy

This app is a tool for accessing a genealogy database you or your family already operate; it is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, since it collects no data itself.

## Changes to this policy

If this document changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

Questions about this app: open an issue at [github.com/Schoaf/webtrees-mobile](https://github.com/Schoaf/webtrees-mobile). Questions about data stored on a specific webtrees server: contact that server's administrator.
